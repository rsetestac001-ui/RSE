Report Title : Geotechnical Manual for Slopes, 2nd Edition (1984), 302 p. (Reprinted, 2011).

Author : Geotechnical Engineering Office

Abstract

The Geotechnical Manual for Slopes provides guidance for the standards of practice that should be adopted for the design, construction and maintenance of slopes and site formation works in Hong Kong.
