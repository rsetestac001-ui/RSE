Report No. : GCO Publication No. 1/90

Report Title : Review of Design Methods for Excavations (1990), 187 p. (Reprinted, 2003).

Author : Geotechnical Engineering Office

Abstract

This document presents the results of a review carried out within the Geotechnical Engineering Office of the state of the art in the design of excavation support systems, and in the prediction of ground movements around excavations. Whilst the document is not intended to be a design guide, the information given here should facilitate the use of modern methods and knowledge in the design of excavation support systems.
