Report No. : GEO Publication No. 1/2011

Report Title : Technical Guidelines on Landscape Treatment for Slopes (2011), 217p.

Author : Geotechnical Engineering Office

Abstract

This Publication presents guidance on good practice of landscape treatments for man-made slopes and engineering works on natural terrain in Hong Kong. It forms part of the continuing commitment by the Government of the Hong Kong Special Administrative Region to make slopes look as natural as possible, with a view to achieving the overall aim of creating a more visually acceptable and ecologically sustainable slope environment.
