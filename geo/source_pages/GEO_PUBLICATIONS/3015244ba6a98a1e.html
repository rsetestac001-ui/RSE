Report No. : GCO Publication No. 5/84

Report Title : Review of Hong Kong Stratigraphy (1984), 86 p.

Author : J. D. Bennett

Abstract

This Report examines aspects of the regional geological framework of Hong Kong as related to southeastern China. The broad stratigraphic and tectonic histories of the region are discussed, and a detailed review is made of the local stratigraphy and its relationship to the regional setting. The report concludes with a summary of current knowledge and provides suggestions for further investigation. The Report is essentially a review of published information available up to September 1983.
