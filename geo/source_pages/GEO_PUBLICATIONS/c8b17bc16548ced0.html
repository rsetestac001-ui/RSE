Report No. : GEO Publication No. 1/93

Report Title : Review of Granular and Geotextile Filters (1993), 141 p.

Author : Geotechnical Engineering Office

Abstract

This document presents a review of principles and practice related to the design of filters for use in drainage systems for earthworks in Hong Kong. Recommendations are made for design of filters, and guidance is given about their construction.
