Amendments to the Explanatory Notes to the Code of Practice on Wind Effects
in Hong Kong 2019 in December 2023 included:

        (a)    Clause  2.2.3 – Addition of  guidelines  for determination of fundamental
             frequency for assessing across wind effects;

        (b)    Clause 2.5 – Addition of design net pressure for hoarding and covered walkway
               associated with construction site; and

        (c)    Clause 4.3.1, Clause 6.4, Figure B-1, Appendices C2 and E3.1 – Textual
              refinement.
