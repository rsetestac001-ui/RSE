Amendments to the Code of Practice for Structural Use of Concrete 2013
(2020 Edition) in June 2023 included:

        (a)    Clause 3.2.8.3 and Annex A – Addition of referenced standard ISO
             15835-2 for the test method on mechanical coupler; and

        (b)    Clause 9.9.1.3(b) and Figure 9.6a – Addition of alternative arrangement
               for links/ties for beam.
