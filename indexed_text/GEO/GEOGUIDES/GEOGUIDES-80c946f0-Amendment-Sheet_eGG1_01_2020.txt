  Notes:  (1) A batch is any quantify of fill material which is from the same type and has

             check whether the compacted backfill conforms to the specified level of

             and moisture contents from which values of relative degree of compaction can
             be calculated.
            (3) Particular requirements for reinforced fill structures are given in Geoguide 6
