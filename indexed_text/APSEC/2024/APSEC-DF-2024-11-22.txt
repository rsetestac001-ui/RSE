BD advised that according to paragraph 4.9 (b) and (c) of the 
Supervision Code, for temporary works, the sequence of construction or 
method statements that were not required to be shown on prescribed 
plans, it would be a case 2 if such temporary works had no effect on the 
permanent structures, adjoining buildings and lands, by way of 
overstressing or overloading, and would be regarded as case 3 if there 
might have such effect.
