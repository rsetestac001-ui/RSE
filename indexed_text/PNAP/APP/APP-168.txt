            Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011


            The Buildings Department (BD) has set up a Technical Committee (TC)
to, among others, collect and consider the views and feedback from the building industry
arising from the use of the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011 (the
Code).  Taking into account the advice of the TC, amendments were made to the Code
in November 2016, May 2021 and March 20231, which have been incorporated in the
Code (2023 Edition) promulgated in March 2023.

2.        On the advice of the TC, the following amendments  to the Code
(2023 Edition) have been promulgated with immediate effect:

          (a)   Appendix A — April 2026.

Amendments to the Code of Practice for the Structural Use of Steel 2011
(2023 Edition) in April 2026 included:

       (a)      Clause 3.1.3 – clarification of the definitions of Ys and Us;

      (b)     Clause 3.1.5 - deletion of the duplicated paragraph;

       (c)      Clause 3.4  - clarification of the welding requirements for welding
                different grades of steel;

      (d)     Clause 5.3.4 – revision of the requirements for building acceleration
                limits;

       (e)      Equation 9.9 – amendment of the typo relating to lap joints;

       (f)      Equation 9.10 – clarification of the definition of net cross sectional area;

      (g)     Clause 10.1.2 and Table 10.1 – addition of information on the short-term
                elastic modulus of concrete;

      (h)      Figure 10.17 – classification of composite columns;

        (i)      Clause 10.5.1(3) – revision of allowable concrete and steel grades in
             composite columns;

        (j)      Clause 10.5.2(5) – addition of requirements for quality control of
              concrete in composite columns composed of concrete filled steel hollow
               sections;

      (k)     Table 10.11 – update of maximum values on geometric ratios;

        (l)      Clause 10.5.3.2 – amendment to the design strengths of concrete and
                steel in composite columns;

     (m)     Table 10.13 – update of buckling curves and member imperfections for
             composite columns;

      (n)      clause 10.5.3.3 – update of the requirements for checking buckling
               strength of composite columns;

      (o)     Clause 13.6.4 – addition of requirements for vibration response of
               footbridges;

      (p)      Clauses 14.3.2 and 14.3.3 – addition of the requirements for mechanised
            and automatic welding;

      (q)     Table 14.2b – revision of the definition for carbon equivalent value;

       (r)     Annex A1.4.2.2 – addition of the acceptable standards for mechanised
            and automatic welding; and

       (s)     Annex  A2.5  –  addition  of  general  reference  for  vibrations  of
              steel-framed structural systems.
